HOW TO PLAY

Each ladoo has a glowing letter. Type that letter to grab it. Missed ladoos reduce health.

  • ⚡ Rapid Fire: makes the grab animation snappier.
  • 🛡️ Shield: absorbs one missed ladoo.
  • ×2 Double Score: doubles points temporarily.
  • 🔥 The higher your score, the faster and denser the rain.

On phones, use the three touch buttons at the bottom of the game.
